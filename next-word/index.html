The mechanism

It predicts the next word.

That is the whole mechanism. Not retrieval from a database. Not reasoning from known facts. Prediction of the most plausible continuation of your text.

A wrong answer and a right answer are produced by exactly the same process — and they arrive looking exactly the same.
