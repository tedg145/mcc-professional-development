The line

Green, yellow, red.

GREEN — ANY TOOL

Already public

Published catalog and marketing text. Public policy language. Generic drafting with no institutional data.

YELLOW — LICENSED TOOLS

Internal, not personal

Draft procedures, agendas, internal communications, planning documents, aggregate enrollment. Signed in with your MCC account.

RED — NO AI TOOL

About a person

Student records · financial aid · health and disability · Title IX, conduct and HR · personnel files · SSNs · credentials · legal hold.

When in doubt, it is red.
