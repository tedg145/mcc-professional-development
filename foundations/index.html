Foundations
What it is, why it fails, what to do about it

Four short activities in order, about an hour in total. By the end you will have a working mental model of what a language model is actually doing, you will have caught a confident answer being wrong, and you will have a repeatable habit for checking the next one. Everything else on this site assumes this hour.
