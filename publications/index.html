Is Language Mathematical?

How Large Language Models Represent Language with Mathematics

Human language is not literally mathematics, but it has enough regular structure that mathematics can model it. This paper walks through how an LLM turns words into tokens and vectors, uses attention to resolve meaning from context, and learns grammar as probability — and why that same machinery explains both its fluency and its mistakes.
