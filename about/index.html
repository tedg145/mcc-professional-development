About the author

Ted Gonzalez, JD/MBA
Director of Physical Plant, McLennan Community College

I have spent more than twenty years on the side of the table where decisions get signed, paid for, and defended — as general counsel, as a chief financial officer, as a partner in a company I helped build, and now running facilities at MCC. That is the lens this curriculum is written through. Not “AI is amazing” and not “AI is dangerous,” but the questions every one of those jobs taught me to ask before anything new goes into daily work: is it allowed, what does it cost, who is accountable, and does it actually hold up?

Four seats, one set of questions

Each role I have held answers a different part of “should we use AI for this?” Together they are why this site teaches judgment before tools.
1

Attorney

General counsel · law faculty

Corporate general counsel for a decade, then contract officer and legal reviewer in higher education. I now teach law at Central Texas College, where I am bringing AI into the curriculum. I know the statutes, MOUs, classified-contract rules and public-records obligations that govern what an institution can do with information.

What I bring to AIWhat can legally go into a tool — FERPA, personnel files, Texas public records — and what the vendor contract really says about your data.
2

CFO

Chief Financial Officer · $102M budget

Chief Financial Officer and Chief of Strategy at Central Texas College. Led the college through a SACSCOC review with no findings and modeled the financial effect of House Bill 8’s new performance funding.

What I bring to AIWhether a tool earns its cost, how to pilot before you buy, and why “it saves time” needs a number attached.
3

Owner

Partner · ~$1.2B in assets

Partner and Vice President of Operations at a start-up student-housing developer that grew to thirty communities across the U.S. and Europe. Owners carry the risk personally, so every process has to work on a Tuesday, not just in a demo.

What I bring to AIAccountability. The tool drafts; a person signs. If you would not put your name on it unchecked, it is not finished.
4

Entrepreneur & builder

Founder · software developer

I write the software, not just the policy. I built every interactive tool on this site, and I founded and engineer two AI products — EX MULTIS, which runs one question through several models and keeps their disagreements, and EGON, a workplace-safety platform.

What I bring to AIHow these systems actually fail from the inside — which is why Foundations starts with how a model works, not with prompts.
